# 推断的范围与因果边界

## 内容

### 原题

**完整原创材料。以下地点、人物与事件均为虚构教学示例，数值不是真实观测。**

For four weeks, volunteers at Willow Stream tested a low-cost water-level sensor beside a footbridge. They compared its readings with marks on a fixed ruler. On calm days, the two methods usually agreed closely. During two windy afternoons, however, waves produced several sudden sensor readings that the ruler observations did not support.

The team added a rule: an alert would be sent only if a high reading continued for five minutes. This reduced the brief alerts in the remaining trial days. It also meant that a very short rise might not produce an alert. A notice beside the bridge asked visitors to follow official safety advice rather than rely on the experimental device.

At the final meeting, a volunteer suggested testing a second sensor upstream. The coordinator supported a longer trial but rejected a proposed headline saying that the project had prevented flooding. “We tested how the device reports water levels,” she said. “We did not compare flood damage with and without it. We also have not tested it through a full season of heavy rain.” The report described the sensor as a possible aid to observation, subject to further checks.

**问题：** 根据全文，哪项推断得到最充分支持？选出一项，并指出其他候选增加了什么原文没有支持的内容。

A. The five-minute rule made the sensor accurate in every weather condition.

B. The revised alert rule reduced brief alerts during the trial, but its usefulness in other conditions still needed checking.

C. The project proved that installing a sensor prevents flood damage.

D. The coordinator opposed all further use of sensors at Willow Stream.

## 参考理解

### 原解

**特征与联想。** 四个候选都提到了材料里的对象，所以只找到同词还不够。要把候选拆成“做了什么、观察到什么、限制在哪里”，再回原文逐项核对。

材料观察到的是：增加持续五分钟的规则后，剩余试行日里的短促警报减少。材料也说短暂水位升高可能漏报，且尚未经历完整强雨季。它没有说修改规则提高了传感器每次测量的准确度，更没有测量灾害是否减少。

所以选 **B**。它保留了观察范围 “during the trial”，把结论限定为短促警报减少，并承接了进一步检验的需要。它没有把一个改进包装成普遍有效。

| 候选 | 可对照的原文 | 超出或符合之处 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | “reduced the brief alerts” 与 “a very short rise might not produce an alert” | 把警报筛选改进扩大成所有天气下测量都准确，混淆了警报规则与测量准确度 |
| B | “remaining trial days”“subject to further checks” | 同时保留已观察结果与尚未检查的范围 |
| C | “did not compare flood damage with and without it” | 把报告水位直接升级为已证明防灾因果，恰与明示限制冲突 |
| D | “supported a longer trial” | 把反对夸大标题误读为反对所有后续使用 |

**步骤怎样迁移。** 先确定候选主语是否与材料同一对象，再检查范围词（every、all、during）、证据动词（suggest、prove）和因果关系。有些词很强并不意味着一定错；如果材料有充分证据，强结论也可能成立。本题排除 A、C、D 的依据是具体内容与证据不匹配，不能简化成“绝对词必错”。

**边界。** B 是这篇虚构短文最支持的推断，不是实际设备的安全认证；“试行需要进一步检查”也不等于设备必然无效。关于真实水域安全，应遵循相应机构的实际指导，不能拿教学材料作现场依据。
